About me

Building innovation cultures & high-impact programs

I connect people, ideas, and institutions to turn ambitious visions into working programs — at scale and across sectors.

I am a program leader with thirteen years of experience spanning startups, academia, and governments. My work sits at the intersection of program development and management, leadership and people management, strategic collaborations, and partnership development.

At Microsoft's The Garage, I lead innovation programs across India — from the Global Hackathon and SkillUp AI series to internal accelerators and internship programs — championing a growth mindset among students, new recruits, and senior leaders alike.

Before Microsoft, I designed national programs for underrepresented communities with the British Deputy High Commission, drove industry outreach and startup incubation at IIIT Hyderabad, and led product innovation at Titan Industries. I care deeply about creating opportunities, nurturing talent, and delivering measurable impact for industry, government, and society.
